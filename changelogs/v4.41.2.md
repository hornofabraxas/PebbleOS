## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.41.2

4 upstream commits since v4.41.1, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.41.2

**Apps / UI**
- Fixed a sizing regression affecting Pebble Time 2 and other round-screen watches: Settings menu rows at the default text size had grown too tall, so the Settings screen showed only three rows instead of the intended four.

**Notifications**
- Reverted a recent change that hid notifications you had already acted on from the notification history and launcher glance, and that stopped dismissed notifications from being re-delivered. Notification history and re-delivery now work the way they did before that change.
