## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.43.0

19 upstream commits since v4.42.0, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.43.0

**Notifications**
- iOS notifications missed while the watch was disconnected (for example during Stand-By Mode, or across a dropped Bluetooth link) are now recovered and delivered once reconnected, instead of silently disappearing.

**Battery / Power**
- Fixed a bug where an interrupted file transfer from the phone could get stuck, keeping the watch's processor busy for hours and draining the battery.

**Bluetooth**
- Fixed a resource leak that built up over certain duplicate Bluetooth connection events.

**Health**
- Sleep tracking no longer stops permanently if the watch's clock is set far into the future and later corrected back; it now resumes logging sleep, naps and walks as normal.
- Fixed a bug where sleep tracking could pause for about an hour around the autumn Daylight Saving Time change.

**Phone & Calls**
- Fixed call timers continuing to run, and leaking resources, when a call was answered directly on the watch.
- Turning Stand-By Mode off from the Pebble phone app now takes effect right away, instead of needing a toggle on the watch, a charger change, or a reboot.

**Apps / Microphone**
- Watchapps can now request permission to capture audio from the microphone directly, for use within the app.
- Watchapps can also stream live microphone audio to their companion app on the phone (the phone has to accept the stream first); a small banner appears at the bottom of the screen whenever an app is listening.

...and about 8 more internal developer-tooling and build commits.
