## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.27.4

2 upstream commits since v4.27.3, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.27.4

This is a fix release for the older 4.27 firmware line, not an update for a watch on newer firmware.

- Fixed a factory aging test leaving the backlight stuck black until reboot, by restoring the original backlight color correctly after the test.
- Fixed the factory idle power-drain test to take its baseline after the battery settles, so units drawing too much idle current are reliably caught.
