## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.9.142.10

1 upstream commits since v4.9.142.9, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.9.142.10

This is a fix release for the older 4.9 firmware line, not an update for a watch on newer firmware.

- Fixed a power management bug that could leave a watch stuck drawing extra battery current after certain restarts, persisting across reboots and firmware updates until the battery was disconnected; affected watches now have this corrected automatically at boot.
