## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.9.142.8

1 upstream commits since v4.9.142.7, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.9.142.8

This is a fix release for the older v4.9.142 firmware line, not an update for a watch already running newer firmware.

- The manufacturing Extras menu now includes a "Clear Bondings" option, with a confirmation dialog, to clear paired Bluetooth devices, matching what was already available on the main firmware line.
