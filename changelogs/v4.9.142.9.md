## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.9.142.9

2 upstream commits since v4.9.142.8, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.9.142.9

This is a fix release for the older 4.9 firmware line, not an update for a watch on newer firmware.

- Fixed the manufacturing battery discharge test so it can retry once after a trickle-charge timeout, instead of permanently failing a battery that just needs more time to recover.
- Added support in the battery charger driver for clearing a latched charger error, so charging can resume after that retry.
