## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.9.142.6

2 upstream commits since v4.9.142.5, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.9.142.6

This is a fix release for the older v4.9.142 firmware line, not an update for a watch running newer firmware.

- PMIC power management: fixed handling of a known power chip hardware anomaly, making the workaround apply more generally instead of just specific cases.
- Fixed a bug where manufacturing-mode firmware incorrectly reported itself as normal firmware to the phone. This could make pairing appear to succeed but then time out before the watch finished connecting.
