## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.42.0

494 upstream commits since v4.41.0, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.42.0

**Pebble Time 2 (obelix)**
- Fixed a crash (HardFault) that could happen when opening the Workout app, caused by recoloring an activity icon stored in read-only flash.
- Fixed the backlight failing to turn on for a wrist flick or tap in dim rooms when the watchface had a dark top-left corner (TicToc, Ultra, Dymaxion, Retro Face all qualify).
- Restored the default menu row height after it had been accidentally shrunk.

**Notifications**
- Quiet Time now supports up to five separate schedules instead of just one for weekdays and one for weekends, each with its own time range and days, and Calendar Aware moved next to Manual in the main Quiet Time menu.
- Acting on a notification from a group now lets you navigate through every notification in that group, not just the one you acted on.

**Workout / Health**
- The Workout app was redesigned with a sports-watch look: a new activity picker, pause/end/complete status banners, labeled data fields, and a heart rate field colored by your current zone.
- Workout summaries now include time spent in each heart rate zone, plus SpO2 readings taken during the workout.
- The Health app's heart rate detail card now shows today's heart rate alongside its history.

**Battery / Power**
- Fixed a crash when shutting down from Settings while the charger was still connected; the watch now simply reports it can't power off instead of resetting.
- Fixed the power chip getting stuck in a higher-power state across reboots once it had entered that state, which could shorten battery life until the battery was disconnected.

**Bluetooth**
- Fixed dictation sometimes dropping audio or failing silently over a laggy connection, and dictation failures now also give a double vibration so they're noticeable without looking at the watch.
- Small interactive replies (like notification action responses) no longer wait behind large Bluetooth transfers such as screenshots or file transfers.

**Display / Performance**
- Watchfaces and apps that only change part of the screen now redraw and send only those pixels over the display link, which should help battery life by cutting down full-screen refreshes.
- Long menu titles that don't fit on screen now scroll sideways instead of being cut off.
- Timeline pin titles in round pin layouts now move together with the pin during scroll animations instead of jumping at the end.

**Apps / UI**
- Weather now abbreviates wind speed in knots as "kt", and the Settings menu now consistently says "SpO2" instead of "blood oxygen".
- Multiple language packs can now be installed side by side, with installed packs listed in the language picker, so a missing character can be filled in from another installed pack.

**Drivers / Reliability**
- Fixed watch resets that could happen under heavy background activity: the system task no longer deadlocks itself when its internal queue fills up, and the task watchdog no longer misfires on threads that are simply waiting their turn.
- Fixed a possible crash reset while a data logging session's unread data grows large, by feeding the watchdog during the copy and speeding the copy up.

**Build / Docs**
- Added a browser-based playground that runs real PebbleOS firmware, including third-party apps, for trying things out without a physical watch.
- Updated the built-in timezone database (DST changes affecting parts of Canada, Moldova and Morocco).

...and about 460 more internal commits (driver and Bluetooth stack refactoring, syscall security hardening, build system and test coverage work, and documentation reorganization).
