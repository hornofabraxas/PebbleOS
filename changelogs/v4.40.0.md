## What's new in upstream PebbleOS v4.40.0

131 upstream commits since v4.39.0, summarized by Claude from the commit messages. Upstream release: https://github.com/coredevices/PebbleOS/releases/tag/v4.40.0

**Bluetooth**
- Fixed a bug where a burst of Bluetooth activity, such as a flood of music or call status updates, could overflow an internal queue and force the watch to reset.
- The watch now tells your phone which notification is on screen, so features like voice replies apply to the right one, and reports when none is showing.
- Notifications your phone re-sends no longer reappear in your notification history after you've already dismissed them.

**Touch**
- Fixed touch input stalling, and the watch occasionally resetting, when a demanding app (such as Dicey Depths) kept the processor busy.
- Covering the screen with your whole palm now turns the backlight off.

**Health**
- Calorie counts on the activity detail screen now show "kcal" so they aren't mistaken for regular calories.

**Apps / UI**
- Several fixes to the accelerometer data apps use, preventing crashes and incorrect sample timing when an app subscribes, unsubscribes, or changes its sampling rate.
- Shake detection now correctly reports which direction the watch was shaken, instead of always reporting the same direction.
- Quick Launch no longer resets the watch if the app assigned to a button has been removed or isn't installed, and closing a voice transcription or dialog popup no longer logs spurious errors.
- QR codes drawn on screen now use far less memory to generate.
- The timeline now reliably catches up when an app is slow to keep up, instead of occasionally resetting the watch.

**Drivers / Reliability**
- The watchdog now gives a stuck app its full grace period before resetting, instead of sometimes resetting immediately.
- Closed two security gaps where a misbehaving app could crash the whole watch or reach accelerometer data it shouldn't have access to.
- Audio and voice playback now always finishes draining before a stream closes, instead of occasionally cutting off early.
- Opening settings and other data files is now much faster, especially for larger files.
- Image decoding used by things like the weather globe now goes through a safer, bounds-checked library.
- Fixed a rendering bug in several system font sizes where accented letters used in Vietnamese and similar languages drew a disconnected bar instead of a proper stroke.
- ...and about 95 more internal commits (native/simulator build support, driver code reorganization, and test coverage).
